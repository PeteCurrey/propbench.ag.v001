## Defining Your Account Risk Budget

Position sizing inside a prop firm evaluation must be governed by maximum drawdown constraints rather than arbitrary fixed lot sizes.
Establishing a strict percentage risk budget per trade ensures account longevity across unexpected losing streaks.

## Calculating Lot Sizes from Stop Loss Distance

Standard position sizing formulas divide your total dollar risk budget by your stop loss distance in pips multiplied by pip value per lot.
This calculation ensures that regardless of stop distance variations, cash at risk remains identical.

## Adapting Risk to Account Growth

As account equity fluctuates, position sizes should adjust to reflect current balance or remaining drawdown buffer.
Reducing lot sizes during drawdown periods prevents compounding losses and protects the account from breach.
