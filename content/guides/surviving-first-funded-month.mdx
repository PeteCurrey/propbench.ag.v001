## Transitioning from Challenge to Live Funded Trading

Transitioning from an evaluation phase to a funded account requires shifting focus from profit growth to capital preservation.
Protecting your starting balance in the first month builds the equity buffer necessary for long-term account survival.

## Building an Initial Equity Buffer

Reduce risk per trade during your first month until you build a 2% to 3% profit cushion above your initial balance.
Building a profit buffer protects your account from trailing or static breach floors before requesting your first payout.

<Callout variant="insight" title="Funded Account Strategy">
Prioritize building a 2% profit buffer before scaling trade size to insulate the account against sudden market volatility.
</Callout>

## Scheduling First Payouts and Risk Scaling

Familiarize yourself with your firm's minimum payout frequency (e.g. 14 or 30 days) and fee structure.
Requesting regular payouts while maintaining a residual buffer keeps your risk exposure balanced.
