## Strategy vs Risk Discipline

Statistical modeling shows that profitable strategies fail evaluation challenges primarily due to position over-leveraging and emotional revenge trading during normal loss streaks.
Managing trade size and preserving drawdown buffers is more critical than high win rates.

## Analyzing Streak Variance

Losing streaks are statistically inevitable over a 50 to 100 trade sample size.
Executing trades with excessive risk per position ensures that a standard 4 to 6 trade losing streak results in account breach.

<Callout variant="insight" title="Behavioral Edge">
Sustained compliance requires strict adherence to risk budgets regardless of short-term win/loss sequences.
</Callout>

## Behavioral Guidelines for Challenge Survival

Set strict daily loss limits below the firm threshold and step away from the terminal after hitting daily risk limits.
Treat evaluation accounts with identical risk discipline as live capital.
