## Understanding Single-Day Profit Caps

Consistency rules specify that no single trading day can represent more than a defined percentage (e.g. 30% or 50%) of your total evaluation profit.
Firms enforce this rule to evaluate consistent trading performance rather than single high-leverage windfalls.

## Mathematical Impact on Evaluation Planning

If a single day accounts for 45% of your profit under a 40% consistency rule, you must continue trading to generate additional profits on other days to restore compliance.
Distributing your profit targets evenly across multiple trading days prevents consistency breaches.

Hugging a single big win requires earning further compliant profits across subsequent trading days to meet firm rules.
</Callout>

## Best Practices for Consistent Returns

Aim for steady, modest daily gains rather than risking large position sizes to hit a target in a single session.
Spreading profit targets over 10 to 20 trading days keeps account variance low and supports compliance.
