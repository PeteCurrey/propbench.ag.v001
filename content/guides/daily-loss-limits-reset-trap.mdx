## Daily Loss Limit Calculation Mechanics

Daily loss limits reset every 24 hours based on your equity or balance at the firm's designated daily reset time (commonly 17:00 New York / ET).
Your breach threshold for the upcoming trading day is fixed at that exact moment.

## The Reset Time Trap Explained

Holding open trades through the daily reset time can cause floating losses to count against your new daily loss allowance.
If floating drawdown carries into a new day, your effective risk buffer for that session is immediately reduced.
