## Key Rule Metrics to Verify

Before purchasing an evaluation account, thoroughly inspect the firm's official terms for drawdown types, daily loss formulas, and reset times.
Rule parameters vary widely across firms and directly impact account survival odds.

## Verifying Source Attribution & Dates

Firm rules are updated frequently. Ensure all figures you rely on are cited from live official terms with clear verification timestamps.
Never rely on unverified third-party promotional claims or marketing summaries.

<Callout variant="framework" title="Verification Checklist">
Always confirm drawdown measurement basis (balance vs equity), reset timezone, and payout schedule in official T&Cs prior to entry.
</Callout>

## Red Flags & Risk Disclosures

Be cautious of unpublished or ambiguous rules regarding news trading, weekend holding, or lot size restrictions.
If a rule is not explicitly published in the firm's T&Cs, treat it as unknown and confirm with support.
