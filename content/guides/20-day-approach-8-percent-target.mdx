## Deconstructing the 8% Target

Achieving an 8% total gain over 20 trading sessions requires an average daily return of 0.4% per session.
Breaking down large evaluation targets into tiny daily milestones eliminates impulse trading.

## Structuring Daily Profit Milestones

Pacing your progress over 20 sessions keeps position risk small (e.g. 0.5% per trade), ensuring drawdown buffers remain untouched.
A linear target plan accommodates normal drawdowns while keeping overall progress on track.
